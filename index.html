METRICS DESCRIPTION

  • AVG distance to travel per day (Km): Total distance traveled divided by the total number of working days.
  • AVG service time at customer (min): Total time spent at outlets divided by the total number of visits.
  • Route Compliance Rate (%): The percentage of visits made to planned ("in-route") customers over total visits.
  • Active Outlet (Customers): The unique number of customers who placed at least one valid order.
  • Active Outlet (%): The percentage of unique active outlets out of the total unique visited customers.
  • Visit Conversion Rate (%): The percentage of active outlets achieved per total store visits (measuring visit efficiency).
  • AVG SKU / Invoice: The average number of SKUs sold per valid invoice/order.
  • AVG Value / Invoice: The average revenue generated per valid invoice/order.
  • AVG Sales per customer (VND): Total sales divided by the total number of visits made.
